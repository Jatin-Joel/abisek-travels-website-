Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Just send us a WhatsApp message with your pickup location, destination, and travel date. We'll reply with an exact quote — usually within a few minutes. No obligation to book after asking.

For local Pathankot trips, same-day booking is usually fine. For outstation trips — especially during peak season (May–June, Oct) — booking 2–3 days ahead is recommended to guarantee availability.

Cancellations more than 24 hours before departure incur no charge. For last-minute cancellations (within 24 hours), a small fee may apply depending on the trip. We always try to be flexible — just let us know as early as possible.

We accept cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), and bank transfers. Payment is usually settled at the end of the trip. For multi-day tours, a partial advance may be required at the time of booking.

The Maruti Suzuki Ertiga comfortably handles 3–4 large suitcases in the boot. If you have a large group or extra camping gear, just mention it when booking so we can plan accordingly.
