Common Questions

What are your rates for outstation trips? +
Rates depend on the distance, route, and vehicle. We always share a clear quote before you confirm. Contact us for an estimate for your specific route.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the trip get a full refund of the advance. Cancellations after that may forfeit the advance. Please confirm details at the time of booking.
How much luggage can I bring? +
The Innova Crysta can comfortably fit 3–4 large suitcases. The Ertiga fits 2–3. If you have a lot of luggage or camping gear, let us know when you book and we'll plan accordingly.
Do you carry oxygen for high-altitude sickness? +
We can arrange a portable oxygen cylinder for Ladakh and Spiti Valley routes on request. Please ask when booking.
Do I need permits for Ladakh or Rohtang? +
Yes — inner-line permits for Ladakh and the Rohtang permit are required and are the traveller's responsibility. We'll guide you through the process and ensure the vehicle has the correct documentation.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, and cash. A partial advance is usually collected at the time of booking for multi-day trips.
Where do you pick up from? +
We pick up from Pathankot (junction, cantt, or your door), Amritsar airport, Chandigarh station, and Delhi NCR. Just let us know your starting point.
Do you drive at night on mountain roads? +
For safety reasons, we don't drive on high mountain passes at night. We plan routes to ensure you reach high-altitude sections during daylight hours. Night driving is fine on highways.
